# Phase 4 — Staff Ops

## Cilj

Otvoriti mobilni staff workspace kroz permission-gated ekrane koji pokrivaju glavne operativne tokove za Writer, Komentator, Moderator i Administrator uloge.

## Tokovi

- Writer: news submit, gallery submit
- Komentator: live desk, event/comment controls
- Moderator: approve queues
- Administrator: users, settings, membership, ticket validate, media upload/pick, dashboard, inbox review

## Web-only

- CMS page builder
- drugi heavy builder/workbench ekrani koji nisu phone-first

## Gate

- ni jedan staff ekran nije dostupan bez permissiona
- backend permission keys ostaju jedini source of truth
